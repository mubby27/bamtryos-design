Why BamtryOS

Everything Web3 needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Built from first principles around user sovereignty — your keys, your identity, your data.

🪪
Universal DID Identity
One W3C Decentralised Identifier replaces every username and password. Your identity works across every chain, every dApp, every device — forever.
🔗
Multi-Chain Wallet
Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Cosmos, Bitcoin — one wallet, one seed, one interface. The Protocol Bridge handles chain-specific complexity invisibly.
🤖
Embedded AI Agent
GPT-4o powered assistant that understands your portfolio, proposes transactions in plain English, and screens every action for risk before you confirm.
📦
Encrypted Storage
Files encrypted on your device before they touch IPFS. AES-256-GCM encryption with keys that only you hold. Share access to any DID at any time.
🔐
Hardware-Backed Security
Private keys live in TEE (Secure Enclave / Android Keystore / TPM). They never leave your device. No server stores your credentials — ever.
🛠️
Universal SDK
Build once with @bamtry/sdk and deploy to Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux. One TypeScript API for all BamtryOS capabilities.
